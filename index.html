Project Background

About Matsonnet Studio

Matsonnet Studio is an AI research workspace built entirely around OpenMM, designed to make real molecular dynamics accessible through plain-language interaction — describe a system, and get an actual simulation, trajectory, and analysis back rather than a canned demo.

The project pairs a live OpenMM backend with an interactive front end: the Engine panel runs genuine physics on request, the Visualizer replays the resulting trajectory frame by frame, and the surrounding tools explain the underlying concepts as you go.
